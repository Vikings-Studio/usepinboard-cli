# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community.

Participants must:

- communicate constructively and focus criticism on ideas and code;
- respect privacy, consent, and security boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or sexualized conduct;
- avoid publishing private reports, credentials, or user data;
- accept maintainers' decisions on safety and project scope.

Unacceptable behavior may result in content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion. Report conduct issues privately to `teamvikingstech@gmail.com`. Maintainers will handle reports confidentially and avoid conflicts of interest where possible.
